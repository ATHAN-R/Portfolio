About the Project

Building a Medieval Adventure

A 2D platformer project focused on interactive gameplay, combat, environmental challenges, and story progression.

Medieval Knight Platformer is a game development project built using Unity and C#. The player takes control of a medieval knight who must travel through dungeon levels filled with enemies, traps, and challenging obstacles.

Players navigate the environment by running, jumping, fighting enemies, and interacting with objects. As the adventure progresses, they encounter increasingly challenging situations and work toward completing the knight's rescue mission.

The project combines gameplay programming, character animation, 2D physics, tilemap level design, health management, scene transitions, audio, and dialogue into one game experience.

Problem Solving

Challenges & Learnings

Animation and Input

Coordinating player inputs with animation states requires careful timing. Attacks, movement, and defense must work together without causing unintended animation transitions.

Physics and Collisions

Traps, moving objects, and platforms rely on correct collision and trigger behavior. Testing these interactions helps prevent unexpected damage and movement problems.

Camera and Scene Changes

Camera positioning becomes more challenging when the player moves between dungeon rooms or enters a boss area. Scene transitions need to coordinate player placement and camera movement.

System Integration

Movement, health, traps, enemies, and scene management must communicate correctly. Organizing scripts by responsibility makes debugging and future improvements easier.
