Project Overview

Medieval Knight is a 2D platformer game developed using Unity. The story follows a knight who receives a mission from the king to rescue the princess from a dangerous dungeon.

Players navigate through different areas, defeat enemies, avoid environmental hazards, and use combat abilities to progress through the adventure. The game combines platforming, exploration, enemy encounters, and a boss fight into one medieval-themed experience.

The project focuses on implementing gameplay mechanics, character animations, enemy behavior, health systems, traps, audio feedback, dialogue, and scene transitions to create a more complete playable experience.

Key Features

  • Player Movement: Move left and right, jump across platforms, and navigate the dungeon using keyboard controls.
  • Combat System: Attack hostile enemies using the knight's combat animations and damage mechanics.
  • Enemy AI: Hostile creatures such as goblins and slimes detect the player, move toward targets, attack, receive damage, and can be defeated.
  • Health System: Track the player's health and handle damage from enemies, traps, and falls.
  • Environmental Traps: Avoid falling rocks, spikes, disappearing platforms, and pressure-plate mechanisms.
  • Pushable Objects: Use movable boxes to interact with pressure plates and activate mechanisms.
  • Potions and Power-Ups: Use health potions to restore health and double-attack potions to temporarily improve combat capability.
  • Boss Battle: Fight a powerful boss with its own health system, combat behavior, and battle-room setup.
  • Dialogue and Story: Present the opening story and ending dialogue through character dialogue interfaces.
  • Scene Transitions: Use doors and transitions to move between areas and continue the adventure after completing important encounters.
  • Audio and Settings: Integrate background music, sound effects, and audio settings for a more immersive game.
  • Main Menu and Pause Menu: Provide navigation and pause functionality for the player.

Gameplay Workflow

01. Main Menu
Players begin at the main menu, where they can start the game and access the available settings.

02. Opening Dialogue
The opening dialogue introduces the king's request and establishes the knight's mission to rescue the princess.

03. Dungeon Exploration
Players traverse platforms, move between areas, jump over obstacles, and explore the dungeon.

04. Enemy Encounters
The knight fights hostile mobs, manages health, and uses attacks to defeat enemies encountered throughout the level.

05. Traps and Interactions
Players avoid hazards such as spikes and falling rocks, react to disappearing tiles, and use pushable boxes to activate pressure plates.

06. Potions and Power-Ups
Potions provide additional support during combat, including health restoration and increased attack capability.

07. Boss Fight
The knight faces a stronger enemy that requires careful movement, attacks, and health management.

08. Completion and Ending
After defeating the boss, the player can progress through the transition door toward the concluding part of the adventure.

Challenges & Development

One challenge was coordinating the player's movement and animations. Walking, jumping, attacking, defending, and taking damage require appropriate animation states and transitions to keep the character's behavior consistent.

Enemy behavior and combat also require careful coordination. Detection ranges, attack distances, cooldowns, hitboxes, health values, and damage feedback must work together for combat to feel responsive.

Environmental traps introduced additional challenges because collision detection and trigger behavior must distinguish between the player and other objects. Pressure plates, pushable boxes, and disappearing platforms require reliable interaction logic.

Another consideration was coordinating the boss-room encounter with the camera, transitions, health UI, and rewards. These systems need to work together without disrupting gameplay.

Audio, dialogue, and scene transitions also contribute to the overall experience. Background music, sound effects, menus, and story sequences help connect the gameplay systems into a more cohesive adventure.
